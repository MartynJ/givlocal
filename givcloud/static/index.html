Savings appear once a day has both inverter readings and Octopus meter readings – usually the following day.

Saving = what the electricity your home used would have cost from the grid on the same tariff, half hour by half hour, minus what you actually paid Octopus after export earnings. Standing charges are the same either way. Days when the home collector was offline for part of the day are marked as estimates.
